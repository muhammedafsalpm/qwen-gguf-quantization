- [ ] Each Kaggle model page has its description, licence (Apache 2.0) and links

- [ ] Medium article read aloud once; anything that doesn't sound like you rewritten

## Reach: what to do and what not to do

- Do: pin the notebook; add the 3 models as inputs to the notebook so it shows on each model page;
  answer every comment; share the links from your profiles.
- Do: mention a real detail in replies (GPU you used, what failed), which keeps the conversation going.
- Don't: ask friends to upvote in exchange, use extra accounts, or post the same link in many unrelated
  discussions. Kaggle removes such votes and can penalise the account.
- Don't: open a public tunnel or chat server from the notebook. Everything stays on 127.0.0.1.
